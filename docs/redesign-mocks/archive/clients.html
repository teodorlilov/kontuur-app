Why this shape
  • No metrics, no posts, no coverage. Those are the Dashboard's and the Calendar's. This tab answers one question the others cannot: is this brand still set up to work?
  • Configuration decays, and nothing currently says so. client_sources.last_fetch_status and social_connections.token_expires_at both exist in the schema and appear nowhere in the product. A dead feed or an expiring token breaks the thing Kontuur sells, silently. That is what the amber and clay states are for.
  • A pillar with no source is a real defect. Because client_sources.pillar_ids maps sources to pillars, and the research pipeline skips pillars with no eligible source, a brand can quietly stop producing a third of its content. Nothing else in the product can detect that.
  • The readiness strip borrows the week strip's grammar — solid, hatched, five slots, labels beneath — so it reads as the same system applied to a different subject, not a second visual language.
  • Identity swatches come from brand_visual_identity, which is configuration rather than content, so brands stay recognisable without smuggling posts back in.
  • Panels, not rows. Each brand carries a sentence of diagnosis, and a sentence does not fit in a table cell — which is the honest reason the ledger kept failing here.
