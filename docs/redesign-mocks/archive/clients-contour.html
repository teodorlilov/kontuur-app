Legibility probe — 11.5px --text3 (#667068) straight over the terrain

The peaks sit at roughly top-right, mid-left and bottom-right in normalised space, so those are the three places the field is densest. This column and the two beside it are deliberately unbacked — no card, no wash — so the strokes are competing with the type directly. If 11.5px secondary text goes muddy anywhere in the app, it goes muddy here first.

Contour strokes are forest at 5–14% alpha. Tertiary ink is #667068, which measures 4.51:1 on warm paper and 5.00:1 on near-white — so the ground change slightly helps contrast, and the field is what claws it back. Scroll this block under the heaviest lines and check the thin captions, not the headings.

Every fifth contour is drawn at 1.1px instead of 0.7px, which is the line most likely to read as an underline or a table rule where it crosses text. Watch for that specifically — it is the failure mode that survives a screenshot review and only shows up in use.

13px body over the same ground, for comparison — this size should never be in question, and if it is, the field alpha is too high rather than the ink being too light.

What to decide
  • A · today — warm paper #f1f0ea, two radial glows, card is a --raised gradient with --sh-card. This is what is on /clients right now.
  • B · clearing — near-white #fbfcfa + contour field. The card stays, but goes opaque with a hairline edge and no shadow: it separates by interrupting the terrain instead of floating above it.
  • C · ledger — same ground, no container at all. Rows sit straight on the terrain, per dashboard-v2: "cards hold unlike things; ledgers hold like things … ledger rows are transparent and the terrain runs straight through."
  • The dark band is identical in all three — it is a clearing either way, and it is here so you can see a dark surface and a light one coexist on the field.
  • Move the pointer: the peak layer eases contour rings toward the cursor and stops within a few frames of it halting. Tick reduced motion and it should vanish entirely, not merely slow down.
  • Type is SF/Segoe here, not Geist — this mock is about ground, not type.
